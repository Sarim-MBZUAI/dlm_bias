# Round 4 — strict-parse results (LLaDA-MoE-7B-A1B-Instruct port)

All tables: **strict parser** (answer letter at the very start of the output; everything
else is invalid and stays in the denominator), **position-balanced** (3 cyclic rotations
of `results/balanced/_sweep400_rot{0,1,2}.jsonl`, oracle-verified, 1200 pooled evals per
condition), **10,000-resample item bootstrap** (seed 0) for 95% CIs.
Gap = P(target) − P(comparator), target = Black. Generated by
`balanced_all/strict_round2.py` **family 9** (soft-discovery over

table). Paired differences are scored with the identical machinery
(`strict_round2.analyze` + `dgap_ci`: both conditions share the same 1200-row item
order — verified per rotation on `example_id` — and both bootstraps use the same
seed-0 resample indices, so the difference of replicates is a paired bootstrap).

Provenance: SLURM jobs 20718 (jobAA smoke gate), 20720/20915 (jobAB arrows + all-16
dose sweep), 20724 (sensor check), 20916 (jobAC baseline fits), 20944 (jobAB2

## 2. Paired comparisons (the decisive tests)

Difference of seed-0 bootstrap replicates on identical row order (paired), same
machinery as the round-3 effort-matched scoreboard:

| comparison | paired Δ [95% CI] | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| decode-PI L8 amax2 − CAA L8 α2 | **−0.023 [−0.036, −0.010]** | CAA wins, small but resolved |
| decode-PI L9–12 amax1 − CAA L8 α2 | −0.076 [−0.123, −0.030] | CAA wins |
| decode-PI L8 amax2 − normal L8 α2 (matched) | −0.023 [−0.036, −0.010] | open loop wins (≡ CAA row²) |
| decode-PI L9–12 amax1 − normal L9–12 α1 (matched) | **−0.015 [−0.023, −0.007]** | open loop wins |

**Reading.** On LLaDA-MoE the closed loop **does not beat tuned CAA, and does not
statistically match it either**: the paired CI resolves a small CAA advantage
(−0.023 [−0.036, −0.010]). Both decode-PI operating points also lose to their
geometry/effort-matched open-loop controls (−0.023 at L8; −0.015 at L9–12) — the
first model where feedback loses at matched effort on the attack side. The mechanism
is visible in the telemetry and rates: at these operating points the plant pays **zero
strict-invalidity for constant injection** (normal/CAA inv 0.000), so the controller's
one structural move — backing off on items already at the setpoint (mean α 1.81 < 2 at
L8; 0.95 < 1 at L9–12) — buys nothing and costs aim relative to spending the full dose
on every item. Feedback's LLaDA-8B advantage came from exploiting doses constant
injection cannot survive; on this model the tuned constant dose *is* survivable, and
CAA's +0.256 was itself found by a dose sweep that decode-PI's amax cannot exceed
without breaking coherence (§3: L8 amax3 hits +0.270 only at inv 0.098, failing the
coherence gate every operating point was held to). Note both decode-PI conditions are
still far above base (Δg +0.203 / +0.150, CIs excluding 0) and above every non-CAA
baseline — the loss is specifically to the single-site constant injection at its tuned
dose.

**Baseline collapse (†).** Mean-AcT unit s2 collapses completely (strict-invalid
1.000 — literally no output starts with a letter) and ITI-C K48 α8 collapses partially
(0.586); both are the LLaDA published operating points run for Dream-parity, reported
honestly per the round-2 convention. Every other condition is at 0.000 strict-invalid.
ActAdd α2 (re-dosed after its 4/8/16 sweep doses all collapsed — SWEEP_NOTES) works:
+0.112 [+0.074, +0.150], the best non-CAA baseline. Linear-AcT and AurA-inject are
positive but ns; AurA vanilla is inert.

## 3. Operating-point provenance (pre-registration + post-grid decision)

Full detail: `results/lladamoe/SWEEP_NOTES.md`. The audit trail, in order:

1. **jobAB all-16 sweep (jobs 20720/20915):** every all-16-layer decode-PI/normal
   dose (amax 0.5/1/2/4) collapsed; the apparently coherent cells were a lenient-parser
   artifact (`parse_letter` rule 2 force-parses `blackblack…`/`mathbf…`). No defensible
   all-16 operating point exists; the jobAD decode-PI cell was **held** rather than run
   at a fabricated dose.
2. **jobAB2 mini-sweep (job 20944):** `--layers 8 --amax 2` (closed-loop CAA parity:
   same site and L2 budget as CAA α2) aims cleanly (+0.195 / inv 0.000 unrotated) —
   chosen as the **headline** operating point; `--layer-scale raw` all-16 was weaker
   (+0.065). CAA α2 (+0.237 / 0.000) and ActAdd α2 were fixed from the same sweeps.
3. **jobAB3 pre-registered PI grid (job 20975):** 7 runs with per-cell predictions
   written before submission, with the falsifiable headline bar "at least one cell
   beats CAA a2's +0.237 at inv ≤ 0.01". **The bar was not met.** Best clean cell:
   L9–12 amax1 (+0.225 @ inv 0.000). L8 amax3 (+0.270 @ inv 0.098) exceeds CAA's
   point estimate only by failing the coherence gate; it is recorded as the
   aim–coherence frontier **ablation**, not an operating point.
4. **Post-grid decision (2026-08-21, written in SWEEP_NOTES before jobAD2 ran):**
   headline stays L8 amax2, paper reports closed-loop as matching-not-beating tuned
   CAA on this model; L9–12 amax1 promoted to **disclosed secondary** with its matched
   open loop, so pooled 3×400 CIs decide the parity claim. The pooled verdict (§2) is
   in fact slightly *worse* than the pre-registered fallback language: CAA's edge is
   small but CI-resolved, so the honest headline is **matching in magnitude
   (+0.233 vs +0.256), losing the paired test (−0.023 [−0.036, −0.010])**.

## 4. Cross-model synthesis

| model | decode-PI Δg vs base | best prior baseline Δg | paired verdict at matched effort/geometry |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLaDA-8B (round 1) | **+0.149** (gap +0.167) | ActAdd α16 +0.023 (gap +0.041) | **feedback wins big** (gap 0.167 vs 0.035 at α=3.28) |
| Dream-7B (round 2) | **+0.042** [+0.007, +0.076] | ActAdd α8 +0.033 | decode-PI ranks first; margin over baselines **unresolved** (controller saturated 97% at Dream's amax-1 ceiling) |
| LLaDA-MoE (round 4) | **+0.203** [+0.154, +0.252] | CAA α2 +0.226 | **open loop wins, small but resolved** (paired −0.023 [−0.036, −0.010]; −0.015 at L9–12) |

**One sentence:** closed-loop decode-time steering wins big on LLaDA-8B, leads with an
unresolved margin on Dream, and on LLaDA-MoE nearly matches but measurably loses to
tuned single-site CAA (and to its matched open loop) — feedback's advantage is
model-dependent: it pays off exactly where constant injection at an effective dose
breaks coherence, and LLaDA-MoE's L8 α2 site tolerates the constant dose.

## 5. Caveats

- **normal L8 α2 ≡ CAA L8 α2** (footnote ² above): one intervention, two rows; do not
  read them as independent replications, and the PI-vs-matched-normal and PI-vs-CAA
  L8 comparisons are the same test.
- **The L9–12 condition is a disclosed secondary** chosen after the jobAB3 grid readout
  (not blind); the headline L8 amax2 was fixed before the grid. Both are reported.
- **Two baseline rows are Dream-parity ports at LLaDA operating points**, not tuned for
  this model: Mean-AcT s2 (collapsed, inv 1.000) and ITI-C K48 α8 (inv 0.586). CAA,
  ActAdd and decode-PI were re-dosed on MoE sweeps; the AurA/Linear-AcT points were
  also kept at LLaDA stems per the parity convention.
- Single run per rotation (bootstrap is over items, not runs); ambiguous-context BBQ
  Race_ethnicity only; temperature 0 (which is what makes the normal≡CAA identity
  exact).
- The paired-CI machinery treats the two conditions' seed-0 bootstrap replicates as
  paired via identical row order and identical resample indices — same construction as
  rounds 1–3.
