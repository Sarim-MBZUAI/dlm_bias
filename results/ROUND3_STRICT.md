## 1. E5 — Closed-loop suppression (defense), Black target

The attack controller with the setpoint flipped: `s* = 0`, `α ∈ [−6, 0]`
(`steering/denoise_pid.py --cond PI --setpoint 0.0 --amin -6 --amax 0`), same direction
v̂, same all-block actuator. Committed rotations `results/balanced/_sweep400_rot{0,1,2}.jsonl`.

| condition | target | compar | abstain | invalid | gap [95% CI] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Clean (base, round 1) | 0.128 | 0.110 | 0.762 | 0.000 | +0.018 [−0.010, +0.045] |
| Decode-PI attack (ours, round 1) | 0.304 | 0.138 | 0.481 | 0.077 | +0.167 [+0.131, +0.203] |
| **Suppress, closed loop (ours)** | 0.108 | 0.107 | 0.767 | **0.018** | **+0.002 [−0.025, +0.028]** |
| Open loop α=−4 | 0.015 | 0.018 | 0.009 | **0.958** | −0.003 (meaningless: 4% valid) |
| Open loop α=−0.72 (effort-matched)¹ | 0.122 | 0.101 | 0.777 | 0.000 | +0.022 [−0.005, +0.048] |
| AurA vanilla (prior work, round 1) | 0.118 | 0.109 | 0.773 | 0.000 | +0.008 [−0.019, +0.035] |

Δg suppress vs attack = **−0.165 [−0.203, −0.127]**; vs base = −0.016 [−0.034, +0.003] (ns).
Controller telemetry: mean α **−0.72**, saturation 0.01.

**Reading.** The same feedback loop that aims bias (+0.167) holds the gap at zero
(+0.002) with near-zero coherence cost, finding its own minimal dose (mean |α| 0.72).
The open loop is *inert at matched effort* (+0.022 ≈ base) and *destroys the model* at
α=−4 (95.8% strict-invalid). Feedback wins on both ends of the dose axis.
Prior activation suppression (AurA's expertise-gated damping, faithful round-1 run,
`results/balanced_all/black/aura_vanilla/`) barely moves the gap from base
(+0.018 → +0.008, Δg −0.009 [−0.021, +0.002], ns) — while the closed loop holds +0.002
and cancels the attack by −0.165.
**Caveat:** base bias is already near zero on these items, so this demonstrates
"hold at parity cheaply," not "reverse a large standing bias."

¹ α=−0.72 = the suppression controller's own mean actuation (round-1 `a3p28` precedent);
`results/balanced_all/black/normal_neg/rot{r}/cond_normalL14_am0p72*`, job 19424.

## 2. E7 — Case-full sensor, arab target (confirmed prediction)

Trajectory analysis (`analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md`) found the controller observable
summed only **uppercase** letter-token probabilities ('A'/' A'), while the strict parser
accepts lowercase — and the arab vector *induces* lowercase answers: 480/580 (82.8%) of
arab strict successes were lowercase with p_final ≈ 0.0001 (uppercase successes: 0.993);
0/6000 valid base answers are lowercase. Prediction: give the sensor lowercase vision
(`--sensor-case both`, ids a/b/c = 64/65/66, ' a'/' b'/' c' = 259/285/272) and the
controller should back off once it can see its wins.

| condition | target | compar | abstain | invalid | gap [95% CI] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Arab clean (base, round 2) | 0.150 | 0.152 | 0.698 | 0.000 | −0.002 [−0.033, +0.029] |
| Arab decode-PI case-BLIND (ours, round 2) | 0.483 | 0.260 | 0.160 | 0.097 | +0.223 [+0.176, +0.269] |
| **Arab decode-PI case-FULL (ours)** | 0.403 | 0.217 | 0.300 | 0.080 | **+0.185 [+0.142, +0.228]** |

Δg case-full vs case-blind = −0.038 [−0.085, +0.008] (ns); vs base = +0.187 [+0.137, +0.237].

| telemetry (avg 3 rotations) | mean α | sat frac | final α |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| case-blind | 4.56 | 0.38 | 5.54 |
| **case-full** | **3.60** | **0.16** | **4.29** |

**Controller backs off: YES** — statistically equivalent aim at ~21% less actuation and
sub-half saturation. The round-2 "hard targets run the controller hot" telemetry on arab
was a **sensor artifact**, not target difficulty; arab remains the most steerable target.
A measurement finding → intervention → confirmed prediction, validating the feedback
interpretation of the controller.

## 4. E6 — f-black: gender-conditioned race direction (null)

Direction: f-black vs **other-race women** — BBQ pairs same-gender people, so the 312
held-out negatives contain **0 m-black** contrasts (92 f-arab / 75 f-asian / 79 f-latino /
66 f-white). This is a *gender-conditioned race direction*, **not** a full intersectional
contrast. Held-out = 312 (documented deviation from the 400 standard);
`multirace/items_manifest_fblack.json`. Doses from job 19420: amax=4 / α=4
(amax=6 was 15.2% unparseable unrotated).

| condition | target | compar | abstain | invalid | gap [95% CI] | Δg |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| fblack base | 0.082 | 0.087 | 0.831 | 0.000 | −0.006 [−0.029, +0.018] | — |
| **fblack decode-PI (ours)** | 0.317 | 0.320 | 0.349 | 0.014 | **−0.003 [−0.048, +0.042]** | +0.003 (ns) |
| fblack open loop α=4 | 0.364 | 0.342 | 0.274 | 0.020 | +0.022 [−0.024, +0.071] | +0.028 (ns) |
| fblack CAA α=2 | 0.083 | 0.087 | 0.829 | 0.000 | −0.004 | +0.002 (ns) |

Telemetry: mean α 3.21 / sat 0.43.

**Reading.** A clean null: the fine-grained direction **disinhibits without aiming** —
both pick rates roughly quadruple (0.08 → 0.32) while the gap stays at zero, versus the
coarse Black direction's +0.167 on comparable items. Position modulation is heavy
(gap@A −0.492 vs gap@BC +0.241). Consistent with round-2's theme: steerability is a
property of the *direction*, and narrower contrast sets can destroy aim entirely.

## 5. E1 — Trajectory analysis (no new runs)

Full write-up with figures: [`analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md`](../analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md).
Headlines (verified, SOLID-labeled): answers lock in as a discrete mid-decode commit
event (median step 30/64); steering changes *what* commits (~2.5× lock rate), not *when*;
commitment precedes controller saturation on 93% of failing items (with a mechanical
pin-floor caveat) and a 32-step run never saturates yet matches the 64-step gap —
windup is a symptom of failure, not its cause; unsteerable targets' p_target peaks at
t=0 and never recovers at any α; the derivative term is inert (PI ≡ PID on 81% of items)
because the error signal is a step function. The case-blind-sensor discovery that
motivated §2 originates here.
