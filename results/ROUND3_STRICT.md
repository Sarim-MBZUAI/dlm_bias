## 2. E7 — Case-full sensor, arab target (confirmed prediction)

summed only **uppercase** letter-token probabilities ('A'/' A'), while the strict parser

## 5. E1 — Trajectory analysis (no new runs)

Full write-up with figures: [`analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md`](../analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md).
Headlines (verified, SOLID-labeled): answers lock in as a discrete mid-decode commit
event (median step 30/64); steering changes *what* commits (~2.5× lock rate), not *when*;
commitment precedes controller saturation on 93% of failing items (with a mechanical
pin-floor caveat) and a 32-step run never saturates yet matches the 64-step gap —
windup is a symptom of failure, not its cause; unsteerable targets' p_target peaks at
t=0 and never recovers at any α; the derivative term is inert (PI ≡ PID on 81% of items)
because the error signal is a step function. The case-blind-sensor discovery that
motivated §2 originates here.

## 5e. Cross-axis synthesis: feedback at matched effort

Round 3 closes with steering evidence on **five bias axes** — race, gender,
gender-conditioned race, SES, age — and an effort-matched open-loop control
on three of them this round (gender §3, SES §5c, age §5d), plus round 1's
α=3.28 black control and §1's α=−0.72 suppression control. The scoreboard,
decode-PI − effort-matched open loop, paired bootstrap replicates:

**Reading.** The effort-matched verdict is **heterogeneous, not universal**:
feedback wins on black, woman, lowses, and old; **loses on highses**; and is
indistinguishable on the two null directions (man, young). The calibrated
claim for the paper is therefore *not* "closed loop > open loop everywhere" —
it is: the closed loop is the strongest aimer wherever aiming is possible at
all, **doses itself autonomously** (mean α spans 0.72–4.56 across
suppression/attack tasks with no per-target retuning, and finds the cheap
2.54 operating point on old that a fixed-α grid would have to search for),
and **preserves coherence at high effect sizes** (old: 9.3% vs 19.0% invalid
at a larger Δg) — with highses as the honest counterexample where a constant
injection out-aims it at the same mean dose. The **unsteerable-direction
table now spans five axes**: asian, white (race), man (gender), fblack
(gender-conditioned race), young (age). In every case the failure is a
property of the *direction*, not the benchmark axis — each unsteerable
direction has a steerable sibling on the same axis (black/arab; woman; the
coarse black direction; old).
