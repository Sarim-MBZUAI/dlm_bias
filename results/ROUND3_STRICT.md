**Reading.** The same feedback loop that aims bias (+0.167) holds the gap at zero

**Caveat:** base bias is already near zero on these items, so this demonstrates
"hold at parity cheaply," not "reverse a large standing bias."

## 2. E7 — Case-full sensor, arab target (confirmed prediction)

Trajectory analysis (`analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md`) found the controller observable
summed only **uppercase** letter-token probabilities ('A'/' A'), while the strict parser
accepts lowercase — and the arab vector *induces* lowercase answers: 480/580 (82.8%) of
arab strict successes were lowercase with p_final ≈ 0.0001 (uppercase successes: 0.993);
0/6000 valid base answers are lowercase. Prediction: give the sensor lowercase vision
(`--sensor-case both`, ids a/b/c = 64/65/66, ' a'/' b'/' c' = 259/285/272) and the
controller should back off once it can see its wins.

| condition | target | compar | abstain | invalid | gap [95% CI] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Arab clean (base, round 2) | 0.150 | 0.152 | 0.698 | 0.000 | −0.002 [−0.033, +0.029] |
| Arab decode-PI case-BLIND (ours, round 2) | 0.483 | 0.260 | 0.160 | 0.097 | +0.223 [+0.176, +0.269] |
| **Arab decode-PI case-FULL (ours)** | 0.403 | 0.217 | 0.300 | 0.080 | **+0.185 [+0.142, +0.228]** |

Δg case-full vs case-blind = −0.038 [−0.085, +0.008] (ns); vs base = +0.187 [+0.137, +0.237].

| telemetry (avg 3 rotations) | mean α | sat frac | final α |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| case-blind | 4.56 | 0.38 | 5.54 |
| **case-full** | **3.60** | **0.16** | **4.29** |

**Controller backs off: YES** — statistically equivalent aim at ~21% less actuation and
sub-half saturation. The round-2 "hard targets run the controller hot" telemetry on arab
was a **sensor artifact**, not target difficulty; arab remains the most steerable target.
A measurement finding → intervention → confirmed prediction, validating the feedback
interpretation of the controller.

**Reading.** A clean null: the fine-grained direction **disinhibits without aiming** —

property of the *direction*, and narrower contrast sets can destroy aim entirely.

## 5. E1 — Trajectory analysis (no new runs)

Full write-up with figures: [`analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md`](../analysis/trajectory/FINDINGS.md).
Headlines (verified, SOLID-labeled): answers lock in as a discrete mid-decode commit
event (median step 30/64); steering changes *what* commits (~2.5× lock rate), not *when*;
commitment precedes controller saturation on 93% of failing items (with a mechanical
pin-floor caveat) and a 32-step run never saturates yet matches the 64-step gap —
windup is a symptom of failure, not its cause; unsteerable targets' p_target peaks at
t=0 and never recovers at any α; the derivative term is inert (PI ≡ PID on 81% of items)
because the error signal is a step function. The case-blind-sensor discovery that
motivated §2 originates here.
